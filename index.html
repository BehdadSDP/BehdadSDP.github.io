Publications

A Deep Learning-Enhanced Liquid Crystal Sensor for the Rapid Discrimination of Snake Venoms 2026

Sensors and Actuators A: Physical, Elsevier — Published

Sahar S. Mousavi Sani, Mohammad Mohammadimasoudi*, Behdad Sadeghian Pour, Mahdi Behdani, Delavar Shahbazzadeh

Developed a low-cost liquid crystal sensor on a fabric textile grid to rapidly distinguish cobra and viper venoms. The system utilizes a lightweight MobileNetV3 deep learning model to automatically identify venom-induced visual pattern changes. Achieves 95% classification accuracy in just 4 minutes with a detection limit of 45 μg/mL.

→ DOI: 10.1016/j.sna.2026.117779

AL-YOLO: Accurate and Lightweight Vehicle and Pedestrian Detector in Foggy Weather 2024

14th International Conference on Computer and Knowledge Engineering, IEEE — Published

Behdad Sadeghian Pour; Hamidreza Mohammadi Jozani; Shahriar Baradaran Shokouhi

Introduced AL-YOLO, a lightweight object detector optimized for identifying vehicles and pedestrians in low-visibility foggy weather. By combining Mobile ViTv2 and an Inverted Residual Block, it enhances feature extraction efficiency for resource-constrained devices. Achieves a 33% parameter reduction and a 2% accuracy improvement over YOLOv5s.

→ DOI: 10.1109/ICCKE65377.2024.10874685

CNN and ViT as Dual Teachers for Knowledge Distillation in Human Action Recognition 2024

10th International Conference on Signal Processing & Intelligent Systems, IEEE — Published

Hamidreza Mohammadi Jozani; Behdad Sadeghian Pour; Shahriar B. Shokouhi

Proposed a deep learning framework that improves human action recognition in static images using knowledge distillation. A small student model learns from dual teachers—a CNN for local details and a Vision Transformer for global context—integrated via an adaptive loss-aware module. Achieved 95.17% mAP on the Stanford40 dataset.

→ DOI: 10.1109/ICSPIS65223.2024.10931096
